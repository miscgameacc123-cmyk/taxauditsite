MSME payment delay: interest and s.43B(h)

Interest under s.16 MSMED Act: three times the bank rate, compounded monthly. Not deductible (Form 3CD clause 22). Under s.43B(h), sums owed to micro/small suppliers and unpaid by year end beyond the 15/45-day limit are disallowed for that year (clause 26).

Assumption: whole 30-day months compound; the remainder accrues pro rata. Days late are counted up to the payment date, or the year end if unpaid.

s.44AB applicability

Thresholds: business ₹1 crore, or ₹10 crore if cash receipts and payments are each 5% or less; profession ₹50 lakh. Confirm the current due dates for the audit report and ITR on the Income Tax portal.
