Tax Audit Suite

Upload your files and the calculations run in the browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

1. Import files

Accepts .xlsx, .xls, .csv, .tsv and .txt (including the caret-separated 26AS text file). Column headings are detected automatically; fix any wrong match using the dropdowns. PDFs must be converted to Excel first.

2. Settings

Interest, s.201(1A): 1% a month from the date tax was deductible to the date it was deducted (or to the date above if never deducted); 1.5% a month from deduction to deposit when deposited after the 7th of the next month (30 April for March). Each month or part month counts as a full month. Late filing fee under s.234E is not calculated.
